## Description

The last item is available, but you can't buy it. Resaf is an AI reservation agent businesses and developers can connect to their reservations, backed by verified PayPal commitments and merchant-controlled rules.

Watch two real AI shoppers compete for one parking space. Gemini chooses structured tools, and AG Grid exposes the competing requests. A confirmed $2 PayPal commitment activates the paid reservation. A separate $25 booking completes, and the accepted business policy returns the $2 commitment through a confirmed PayPal refund. The displayed provider identifiers belong to the same recorded Sandbox round.

The business sets fees, time limits and refund terms. Resaf enforces inventory and timing; PayPal supplies the financial state it verifies. Future commitment fees can rise or fall under configured rules, while an accepted quote stays fixed. An unpaid claim that expires frees the opportunity for another buyer.

The 2026 figures distinguish evidence from illustration: Adobe forecasts $275.1 billion in U.S. online holiday sales; Quantum Metric reports 48 abandoned retail-agent carts per purchase versus about three overall. Neither figure measures lost reservation revenue. The $7,500 monthly example assumes ten otherwise-filled $25 bookings are lost daily across 30 days; it is illustrative, not a measured loss or promised recovery.

All money is PayPal Sandbox test money; inventory is a demo resource. This round uses capture and refund, not an uncaptured authorization hold. The original laptop screen is preserved with localized privacy blur. Narration is AI-generated.
