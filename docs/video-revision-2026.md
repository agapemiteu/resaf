# Resaf presentation research — November 8, 2026

## Current figures

**The RealReal:** The Verge's October 2026 experiment used Meta Muse to hold ten listings on repeated twenty-minute windows throughout the day. The retailer tightened transaction defenses while keeping discovery available.

**Agent conversion:** Quantum Metric's 2026 Peak Season Benchmark reports 48 abandoned retail-agent carts per completed purchase versus about three for retail traffic overall. Its observed agent sessions grew from 46,000 in October 2025 to 1.14 million in September 2026. The report combines aggregated platform observations and a US/UK consumer survey.

**U.S. commerce opportunity:** Adobe forecasts $275.1 billion in U.S. online holiday sales for November–December 2026, a 6.7% year-over-year increase.

**Operator revenue scenario:** Ten additional completed bookings daily × $25 × 30 days projects $7,500 in monthly booking revenue. The graphic labels this a projected scenario and the narration says “could generate.”

## Pitch direction

Move directly from the problem to the opportunity: businesses need a way to turn agent traffic into genuine commitment. Resaf lets them connect financial commitment and bounded access to their existing reservations. Developers can bring that mechanism to their clients' inventory and booking systems.

Preserve the full original laptop frame and chosen suspenseful opening. Show the real Gemini tool calls, AG Grid race, verified PayPal commitment, separate purchase and confirmed refund. Explain the supporting tools during use. Close with “Agents move faster. They shouldn't shut everyone else out.” The closing screen reads “Faster agents. A fair chance for everyone.”
